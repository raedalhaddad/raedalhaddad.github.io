Personal Operations Gmail POC

Private personal use

Personal Operations Gmail POC is a private proof-of-concept application for the operator's own authorized Google accounts. It is not offered for public registration, commercial use, or use by unrelated third parties.

Authorization

The application may access Google account data only after the account owner explicitly authorizes the requested permissions through Google's OAuth consent flow. Authorization may be revoked at any time through Google Account controls.

Permitted functionality

The application is intended to monitor and reconcile Gmail metadata for personal operational purposes. It is not intended to read Gmail message bodies, send mail, modify messages, or delete messages.

Availability

This proof of concept is provided for personal testing and may be changed, suspended, or discontinued at any time. No guarantee of uninterrupted operation or availability is made.

Privacy

The application's handling of Google user data is described in the Privacy Policy.
